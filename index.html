[JH_/_02] What I actually do

Keeping the shoot moving.

01

Capture

I run a fast, organised Capture One workflow, keeping the photographer shooting and the agency and client close to the images as they come in.

  • Capture One sessions, naming and structure
  • Phase One · Hasselblad · Canon · Nikon · Sony · Fuji
  • Live view, focus checks and frame overlays
  • Phase One Certified Professional
02

Colour

I build the look with the photographer and keep it consistent across the shoot, from the first test to the final handover.

  • Calibrated client monitoring
  • Styles, ICC profiles and capture adjustments
  • On-set comps, plates and stitching
  • Selects, exports and client previews
03

Data

Every frame is organised, checked and backed up throughout the day, with a clean handover when we wrap.

  • Three-copy backup workflow
  • ChronoSync mirroring
  • Shot logs, metadata and file naming
  • Screens, power and cable management
